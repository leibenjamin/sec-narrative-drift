# Lab Excerpt Picker UI Smoke Checklist

Use this quick checklist after updating excerpt picker UX.

1. Load the Lab tab for KO 2023-2024 (det_llm_excerpt_picker_v1).
2. Confirm full paragraphs render for all 10 evidence items (or see the fallback warning if unavailable).
3. Confirm the "Paired changes" section shows at least one pair when highlights overlap.
4. Confirm snippet highlights appear inside the paragraph text.
5. Click "Jump to Compare Pane" and verify the year pair loads in the overview compare pane.
